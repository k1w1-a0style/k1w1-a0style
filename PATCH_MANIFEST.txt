PATCH37 - CI hard-fix for EAS Android builds

Replaces:
- .github/workflows/k1w1-triggered-build.yml
- .github/workflows/eas-build.yml

What it does:
- Removes fragile self-heal grep/commit logic.
- Ensures dependencies are installed before running any Expo/EAS commands.
- Deletes incomplete android/ios native directories that break managed EAS builds.
- Generates a local CI keystore for non-production profiles to avoid the "Generating a new Keystore is not supported in --non-interactive mode" error.
- Blocks production builds in CI unless real credentials are already configured.
